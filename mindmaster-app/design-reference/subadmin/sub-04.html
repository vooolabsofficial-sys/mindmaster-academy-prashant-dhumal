Namaskar Aditi! For 96 × 94:

  • Base is 100: Deviations are -04 and -06.
  • Right Side: (-4) × (-6) = +24 (no negative carry here!).
  • Left Side: 96 - 6 = 90 (or 94 - 4 = 90).

Final Answer is 9,024. Please re-check the signs in your rough sheet!
